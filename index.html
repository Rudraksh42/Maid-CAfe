Our Little Love Café ♡
♡ Someday, somewhere in Mumbai ♡

A little place of our own, created with love.

One of my favourite dreams is the thought of us starting a café of our own in Mumbai. Not just a place where people come to eat or drink coffee, but a little place that carries a part of our hearts in every corner.

I imagine us finding a lovely little niche spot, somewhere peaceful, cosy, and full of character — away from all the noise when someone needs a quiet moment, but still filled with laughter, conversations, and the smell of freshly brewed coffee.

I want our café to feel safe and welcoming for everyone. A place where people can be themselves without feeling judged, where kindness matters, where everyone is treated with respect, and where even someone having a difficult day can find a little comfort.

Maybe there will be warm fairy lights, cute little tables, flowers by the windows, books to read, anime decorations, our favourite desserts, and a special menu that reminds us of our own story. Maybe we'll have our own little corner where we can sit after a long day and look at everything we built together.

And the most beautiful part? It wouldn't just be about running a business. It would be about creating something meaningful together — a place built on love, care, respect, and the belief that everyone deserves a little happiness.

Someday, I hope we can look around our little café in Mumbai, see people smiling, and say to each other: "We dreamed of this together, and we made it real." ❤️

Our dream. Our café. Our little universe. ♡
Sealed with all my love

A Little Letter for You 💌

Some things are easier to say when they're written from the heart.

💌

To my favourite person, Charmy

A tiny letter, written only for you.

Hey, Charmy. ❤️ I wanted to give you something a little different. Not just a text on your phone, not just another gift, but a tiny world that belongs to us. Three years. So many memories. So many conversations, laughs, little moments, and things that only we understand. I know things haven't always been perfect. I know there are times I could have listened better, understood you better, and shown you the love you deserve in the ways you needed. I'm sorry for those moments. But there's something I never want you to doubt: you matter to me. Your happiness matters. Your feelings matter. And the little things about you that you might not even notice are the things I adore. If our story were an anime, I'd choose all the ordinary episodes too the quiet conversations, the ridiculous jokes, the moments when nothing special happens except that we get to be us. You don't have to be perfect. You don't have to be happy every day. You can have your difficult days, your doubts, and your own dreams. I want you to feel loved as the person you are. Thank you for being part of my life and for all the memories we've made. This cafe is small, but the feeling behind it is huge. With all my love, Your Rudraksh ♡

20/10/23
